Our research group investigates optical and electronic phenomena in low-dimensional nanoscale materials using ultrafast imaging and laser spectroscopy. We focus in particular on van der Waals (vdW) layered materials, whose atomically thin layers enable precise control of atomic structure, flexible heterostructure design, and moiré engineering. The materials include graphene, transition metal dichalcogenides, hexagonal boron nitride, black phosphorus, and their heterostructures. These unique characteristics provide an ideal platform for exploring strong Coulomb interactions, emergent quantum phenomena, and novel light–matter interactions beyond the limits of conventional semiconductors. Ultrafast laser imaging and spectroscopy, together with the development of advanced device fabrication and material synthesis techniques, form the core experimental approaches of our research.
