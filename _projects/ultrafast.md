Directly observing the motion of charge carriers, excitons, ions, phonons, and atomic-scale structures in materials and operating devices provides fundamental insight into the microscopic processes that govern material properties. We develop novel in situ ultrafast laser imaging techniques that leverage key capabilities of ultrafast lasers, including wavelength tunability for resonant excitation and extremely short optical pulses, enabling real-time visualization of dynamic processes in materials and devices with high sensitivity and temporal resolution.
