Novel light emission and absorption properties of vdW quantum materials opens new opportunities for transformative optoelectronic devices that go beyond the foundations of conventional semiconductor technology. vdW quantum materials enable the realization of ultrathin, highly efficient light-emitting and photodetecting devices. Moreover, their unique electronic structures allow access to new spectral regimes—from deep-ultraviolet to infrared frequencies. We fabricate optoelectronic devices based on vdW quantum materials using established semiconductor processing techniques to electrically control optical responses. In addition, we integrate nanophotonic structures, such as optical cavities, to enhance and tailor light–matter interactions at the device level.
